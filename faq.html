About the products

Do I need any experience with computers or AI?

No. Our materials start from the very beginning. If you can send an email and browse the web, you have all the skills you need to get started.

Who is the “AI at Work” workbook designed for?

It's written for adults 50 and over who want to use AI on real work tasks, such as emails, memos, meetings, spreadsheets and career updates. If you can write an email, you can do everything in it. Each section takes about ten minutes.

Which AI tool does it use?

Any of the main AI assistants works, such as ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot or Gemini. The workbook helps you pick one and stick with it for the four weeks.

Do I have to pay for AI tools to use the workbook?

No. A free account with any of the main assistants is enough. Free accounts do have usage limits, which vary by service.

Do you offer workshops for specific jobs or industries?

Yes, field-specific workshops are on the way. Check the products section for what's available now and coming soon.

Can I buy workbooks for my whole team or organization?

Yes. For group purchases or a live workshop for your team, please contact us for options.

Buying & downloading

How do I receive my purchase?

Right after you pay, you'll see a download page and receive an email with your private download link. Keep that email so you can download your file again later.

What format is the workbook in?

It's a 16-page PDF. You can read it on a computer, tablet or phone, or print it out and fill in the practice spaces by hand.

Is checkout secure?

Yes. Payments are handled by a trusted, secure payment provider. We never see or store your full card details.

I didn't get my download email. What should I do?

First check your spam or junk folder. If it's not there, email us at baedigitalwork25@gmail.com with the email address you used at checkout and we'll send you a new link.

Can I get a refund?

Because our products are digital downloads, all sales are final. If your file is damaged or won't open, we'll gladly send you a working copy. See our Refund Policy for details.

Can I share the workbook with friends or coworkers?

Each purchase is licensed for one person's personal use. Please don't share or resell the file. For groups, contact us about team options.
